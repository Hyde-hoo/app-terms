This policy explains how data is handled when you use the Android game Mahjong Blast from Foxglow or contact us about privacy.

Advertising and data shared with Google

Mahjong Blast uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and transmit the following information to Google, depending on your device, applicable settings and the ad request:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Interactions such as opening the app, tapping an ad or watching an ad video.
  • Diagnostics such as crash, launch and performance information.
  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID, where available and permitted.

Google can use this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics and prevent fraud. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it collects in transit using TLS. See Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosures and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Choices, retention and deletion

You can manage the advertising ID in Android’s privacy or ads settings where that control is available. Google’s data retention policy explains how Google retains and deletes the data it processes.
